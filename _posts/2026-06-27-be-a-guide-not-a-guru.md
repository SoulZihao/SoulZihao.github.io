### 📍 Key Takeaways
* **Write for yourself:** Don't obsess over what others want to read, just focus on what genuinely interests you.
* **Building a serendipity vehicle:** Trust that putting your ideas out into the world will attract unexpected, positive opportunities.
* **Be a guide, not a guru:** You don't need to be an absolute expert to share value; just document your learning journey and help the person one step behind you.
